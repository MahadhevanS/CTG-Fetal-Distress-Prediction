URM Bedside Explainer

Patient-level risk, tied to the same fetal-heart-rate, deceleration, trajectory and maternal-context variables a clinician already reads on a CTG trace — not raw model weights.

Illustrative, not clinical. These are three real CTU-UHB patients chosen to show the extremes of what the locked URM produces (a clear true positive, a clear true negative, and a case it misses) — this interface is a reporting mock-up, not a bedside deployment.
Risk = URM's fused score (TAM trajectory × 0.35 + parity model × 0.65) at the delivery-time window. Feature directions are z-scores against that patient's own canonical training fold — never their own held-out fold. Operating threshold = the frozen 80%-target-sensitivity cut-point (0.210).
